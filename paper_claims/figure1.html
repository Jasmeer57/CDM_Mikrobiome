Microbiome-CDM · Figure 1 · System Architecture

From 352 human gut-microbiome papers to a queryable Knowledge Graph.

A Common Data Model (CDM) and a 7-source hybrid knowledge graph for the human gut microbiome corpus — built by combining a small LLM and four sciSpaCy NER models with a curated biological knowledge base. Validated on 50 papers (out of 352 in `papers_txt/`) with 6 differentiator claims.

1   Substrate what we're modeling

Source dataset
352 local gut microbiome papers
All 352 papers in papers_txt/ form the empirical scope of this paper. A 50-paper subset is the validation cohort for claims A–F.
What we ingest
Paper text + BioProject metadata
Full-text papers in papers_txt/ + the BioProject metadata dump (bioproject_data_export.json, 41 MB) for cross-referencing project metadata.

Motivating reference — not our data

Abdill, R. J., Graham, S. P., Rubinetti, V., et al. (2025). Integration of 168,000 samples reveals global patterns of the human gut microbiome. Cell 188(4):1100–1118.e17. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2024.12.017. — Cited as motivating context for why a microbiome CDM is useful. No empirical claim in this paper is about those 168K samples. The empirical scope of this paper is strictly the 352 papers in papers_txt/.

2   Extract two pipelines, one schema

Pipeline A
LLM extractor  qwen3.5:9b · Ollama
Generative, in-context. CDG prompt asks for 14 entity types. Chunked at 8 KB with no overlap. ~45–370 s per paper, ~0.18 ents/s.
Pipeline B
sciSpaCy extractor  4 models in parallel
en_core_sci_lg + en_ner_craft_md + en_ner_bc5cdr_md + en_ner_jnlpba_md. Discriminative, deterministic. ~0.5–2.7 s per paper, ~634 ents/s.

Text alone is biologically incomplete. KB alone is corpus-blind. Hybrid is both.

The text-only KG (v1) had 0% recall on Crohn's drugs, 0% on gut-disrupting antibiotics, and type errors (Ruminococcus gnavus → PROTEIN, Fusobacterium → CHEMICAL). Adding the curated biological_kb layer (S6 entities, S7 relationships) brought full-triple recall to 100% on 110 gold associations across 12 categories, 6 relation types, all 110 triples matched. Leave-K-out validation confirms: even with 30% of the KB held out, the text-mining layers alone recover 100% of the entities — but only ~28% of the specific relation types. The hybrid is necessary, not optional.

What this paper is — and is not.

This is a methods-and-resource paper, not a discovery paper. The empirical claims are bounded by the 352 local papers; the 50-paper batch is the validation cohort. The biological KB is internal scaffolding (curated in this directory) for type validation, synonym expansion, and biological coverage — not an external reference. The 2,096 KG-driven candidate novel findings are reported as hypotheses, not discoveries — external validation is the next step.
