The gut microbiota composition is significantly altered in patients with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM). Metformin treatment has been shown to increase the abundance of Akkermansia muciniphila and short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) producing bacteria such as Faecalibacterium prausnitzii. 16S rRNA sequencing analysis revealed a decrease in microbial diversity. The ratio of Firmicutes to Bacteroidetes was correlated with BMI. Furthermore, butyrate production was linked to improved insulin sensitivity.
