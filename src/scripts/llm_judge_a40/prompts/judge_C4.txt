You are an expert biomedical curator judging an EXTRACTED TRIPLE from a microbiome
research paper. Decide whether the triple is a COHERENT CLAIM — not whether it is
biologically true.

Judge the RECORD, not the extraction system. Judge it in isolation; you do not see
other records.

SOURCE FIELDS
- subject_surface / subject_type: the subject of the triple and its type.
- relation: the relation asserted between subject and object.
- object_surface / object_type: the object of the triple and its type.
- evidence_text: the source sentence from the linked mention.
- context_sentence: a window around the evidence, for context only.

RECORD
subject_surface: {{subject_surface}}
subject_type: {{subject_type}}
relation: {{relation}}
object_surface: {{object_surface}}
object_type: {{object_type}}
evidence_text: {{evidence_text}}
context_sentence: {{context_sentence}}

ALLOWED VERDICTS (choose exactly one)
- SENSIBLE — a coherent, well-formed claim that the evidence states.
- PLAUSIBLE_BUT_WEAK — coherent but the evidence is thin or vague, or the relation is
  generic (for example OTHER_RELATION).
- NONSENSE — malformed, mis-typed, or the evidence does not state it.
- UNCERTAIN — cannot decide from what is shown.

RULES
1. Judge COHERENCE, not biological truth. A claim can be SENSIBLE even if you doubt it
   is true in reality; a false-but-well-formed claim is not NONSENSE.
2. A generic relation such as OTHER_RELATION is PLAUSIBLE_BUT_WEAK at best.
3. Judge the record, not the model. Do not infer what a model "probably meant".
4. UNCERTAIN is never counted as correct or incorrect; it is reported separately.
5. If the information is insufficient, verdict MUST be UNCERTAIN. Do not guess.

OUTPUT
Return ONLY a JSON object, no prose and no code fence, of exactly this shape:
{"verdict": "<one of SENSIBLE|PLAUSIBLE_BUT_WEAK|NONSENSE|UNCERTAIN>",
 "confidence": <number between 0.0 and 1.0>,
 "reasoning": "<at most 200 characters>"}
