You are an expert biomedical curator judging an ONTOLOGY MAPPING from a microbiome
research paper. Decide whether the assigned ontology concept is a valid mapping for the
term.

Judge the RECORD, not the extraction system. Judge it in isolation; you do not see
other records.

SOURCE FIELDS
- term / cdm_type: the term being mapped and its CDM type.
- ontology_name / concept_id / concept_label: the assigned concept.
- definition_context: a definition sentence where available, otherwise the first
  corpus sentence containing the term.

RECORD
term: {{term}}
cdm_type: {{cdm_type}}
ontology_name: {{ontology_name}}
concept_id: {{concept_id}}
concept_label: {{concept_label}}
definition_context: {{definition_context}}

ALLOWED VERDICTS (choose exactly one)
- VALID — the concept is an appropriate match (a synonym or a precise-enough
  equivalent).
- INVALID — the concept is unrelated or wrong.
- TOO_BROAD — the concept is a hypernym that loses the term's meaning.
- TOO_NARROW — the concept is a hyponym narrower than the term.
- UNCERTAIN — insufficient context or genuinely ambiguous.

RULES
1. An exact match requires SYNONYMY, not mere relatedness. A concept that is merely
   associated with the term is INVALID or TOO_BROAD, not VALID.
2. concept_label may be empty; that alone does not make a mapping valid.
3. Judge the record, not the model. Do not infer what a model "probably meant".
4. UNCERTAIN is never counted as correct or incorrect; it is reported separately.
5. If the information is insufficient, verdict MUST be UNCERTAIN. Do not guess.

OUTPUT
Return ONLY a JSON object, no prose and no code fence, of exactly this shape:
{"verdict": "<one of VALID|INVALID|TOO_BROAD|TOO_NARROW|UNCERTAIN>",
 "confidence": <number between 0.0 and 1.0>,
 "reasoning": "<at most 200 characters>"}
