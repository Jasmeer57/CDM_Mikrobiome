MANUAL REVIEW OF 500 TERM-TO-ONTOLOGY MAPPINGS -- REVIEWER GUIDE (same categories the two model judges used)

Each row is one canonical term from the microbiome corpus (deduplicated across 453 papers), its entity type, and the
ontology concept it was mapped to, with the ontology's own definition (ontology_definition) where the ontology has
one. Taxonomy and model columns are pipeline outputs, shown for context only. Work alone. Do not open KEY_model_verdicts.csv before you finish. You may open
the concept_link to read the ontology's own definition and synonyms; you may use general references. The term is
meant in the sense of its entity type (term_type) in human-microbiome research.

MAPPING (how the CONCEPT relates to the TERM) -- pick one:
  EXACT     same concept (synonym, abbreviation, spelling variant).
  BROADER   the concept is more general than the term (term "Bacteroides fragilis" -> concept "Bacteroides").
  NARROWER  the concept is more specific than the term (term "IBD" -> concept "Crohn disease").
  RELATED   associated but a different concept (term "butyrate" -> concept "butyrate kinase").
  WRONG     unrelated or the wrong sense (a moth genus for a bacterium, a gene for a disease abbreviation,
            a person name).

TERM_IS_CONCEPT:
  Y  the term is a real scientific concept.
  N  it is not: numbers, statistics, p-values, figure or table labels, sentence fragments, author names,
     generic words ("group", "level", "data"), formatting debris.

TYPE_CORRECT:  Y if term_type fits the term (ORGANISM, CHEMICAL, DISEASE, PROTEIN, DNA_RNA, CELL_TYPE,
  SAMPLE_METHOD, LAB_METHOD, DATA_ANALYSIS, PATHWAY, EXPERIMENTAL_METHOD, STUDY_DESIGN, GENOMICS, CLINICAL); else N.

DEFINITION (only if definition_in_paper is filled; otherwise NA):
  DEFINITION  the text defines or expands the term.
  MENTION     the term only appears; the text does not define it.
  WRONG_TERM  the text defines a different term.

NOTES: optional; e.g. the correct concept or type.

Time: about 40-60 seconds per row (500 rows: roughly 6-8 hours; split over several sittings). A second person
annotates the first 100 rows (ANNOTATION_SHEET_B_overlap100.xlsx) independently, to measure agreement.
