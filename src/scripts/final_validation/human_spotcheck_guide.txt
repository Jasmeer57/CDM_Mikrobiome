MANUAL CHECK OF 100 TRIPLES -- ANNOTATOR GUIDE (same definitions the model judge used)

Work alone. Do not discuss items with the other annotator until both sheets are finished. Do not look at any model
output (the KEY file) before both sheets are finished. Judge ONLY from the sentence shown in the row. Do not open the
paper. If the sentence is cut off or unreadable, use NOT_SUPPORTED and write "unusable" in NOTES.

A triple is: SUBJECT -- RELATION -- OBJECT, extracted automatically from the sentence.

SUPPORT (column I) -- pick one:
  SUPPORTED      the sentence states this subject-relation-object claim, in this direction.
  PARTIAL        the sentence conveys it only hedged ("may", "suggests", "possibly"), or with a qualifier the
                 triple drops (e.g. "in mice only", "at baseline"), or one end is a broader paraphrase than the
                 sentence supports.
  NOT_SUPPORTED  the sentence does not state this relation (including when an end of the triple is not in the
                 sentence at all).
  CONFLICTS      the sentence states the opposite (e.g. triple says INCREASES, sentence says decreased).

POLARITY (column J):
  Y  the direction in the relation label (increases / decreases / produces / reduces ...) matches the sentence.
  N  it does not match.
  U  the relation label has no direction (ASSOCIATED_WITH, OTHER_RELATION, PRESENT_IN, ...).

RELATION_JUSTIFIED (column K):
  Y  the sentence's own wording justifies this specific relation label.
  N  a weaker or different label would be needed (e.g. "associated with" does not justify CAUSES).

NOTES (column L): optional, up to ~25 words; quote the decisive phrase ("may", "associated", "produces").

Time: about 1 minute per item, roughly 1.5-2 hours for 100. Save the file under its own name (A or B).
After both are done, both annotators resolve disagreements together and record the agreed label; the analysis
reports each annotator separately, their agreement, and the agreed labels.
