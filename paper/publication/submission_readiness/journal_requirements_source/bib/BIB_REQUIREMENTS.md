## Scope

> "Briefings in Bioinformatics is a review journal and does not publish articles that are pure original research."

Our submission must therefore be framed as a **Problem Solving Protocol**, not as a research article or a data descriptor.

## Article types

| Type | Words | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews | 2000–7000 | "Do not include new results from your own work" |
| **Problem solving protocols** | **2000–5000** | "protocols for solving a specific problem using different sets of programs" |
| Case studies | 2000–5000 | "Case studies in biological research applied to clinical practice" |
| Opinion articles | 500–1000 | |
| Letters to the Editors | 500–1000 | critique of an article in the journal |
| Software and website reviews | 500–1000 | "must be written by authors other than the originators" |

## Manuscript elements

| Element | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Key Points | "should consist of 3-5 brief sentences" | confirmed |
| Keywords | "up to six keywords should be provided for indexing purposes" | confirmed |
| Author biographies | "a short (about 30 words) description of the author(s)" | confirmed |
| Title page | title, authors, affiliations; corresponding author's telephone, fax and email | confirmed |
| Abstract | "a short abstract" (stated for reviews); no length found for protocols | **not confirmed**: check the submission system |
| Figures | 600 dpi for line art, 300 dpi for greyscale. Bar charts need 2 or more categories and at least 5 observations; line charts at least 10 observations; pie charts 4–12 segments | confirmed |
| Figure, table and reference limits | none stated | confirmed: none stated |
| References | numbered in square brackets in order of appearance; with 4 or more authors, the first three then "et al." | confirmed |

## Endmatter and policies

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Data availability | "The Data Availability Statement should be included in the endmatter of your article under the heading 'Data availability.'" |
| Funding | a separate section headed "Funding", before Acknowledgements |
| Conflicts of interest | "It is mandatory for authors to declare any potential conflicts of interest", declared at submission |
| ORCID | required for the submitting author |
| Originality | "original, previously unpublished" and not under consideration elsewhere. A bioRxiv preprint policy was not found on these pages, so check it before posting one. |
| Open access | fully open access; the corresponding author pays an article charge, unless covered by a Read and Publish agreement. The standard charge is **not confirmed**. |

## AI and LLM use

> "It is not acceptable to use LLMs or related technologies to draft papers"

> "Any acceptable use of LLMs or related technologies to produce, or help to produce, part of the text, figures or other contents of the paper should be explicitly declared and documented."

> AI use "should be disclosed both in cover letters to editors and in the Methods or Acknowledgements section of manuscripts."

> "LLMs cannot be listed as authors"

**Consequence for this project:** the drafts in `paper/publication/manuscript_draft/` were drafted with an AI coding assistant. They are working notes only. The submitted text must be written by the authors, and every AI use must be declared (see `manuscript_bib/BIB_MANUSCRIPT_PLAN.md`, section 8).
